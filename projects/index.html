Lead-author study · Preregistration in preparation

Interoceptive Pathways Linking Childhood Maltreatment to Adult Affective, Somatosensory, and Sleep Symptoms

Keegan Whitacre

I am testing whether distress and confusion about bodily sensations help explain associations between childhood maltreatment and adult depression, anxiety, somatic symptoms, and sleep disturbance. The preregistered analysis uses parallel mediation models, with exploratory analyses of socioeconomic context and daily somatic–affective coupling.

Whitacre, K., Feldman, M., Bonar, A., Ma, R., & Lindquist, K.
